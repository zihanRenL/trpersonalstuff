01 — Profile

WHO IS
BEHIND THE SCREEN

ゼロ・パーソナル・インデックス

I came to engineering through psychology. Three years at the University of Washington taught me to ask what a person is actually doing — not what a system assumes they are doing — and to prove the answer with data rather than intuition. I'm carrying that into a master's in Industrial & Operations Engineering at Michigan, where the questions get bigger: how work flows, where it stalls, and what a process costs the people inside it.

My research has lived at the edges — color and emoji accessibility for screen reader users, caregiver stress, crime data and the resources that never quite reach the people who need them. The common thread is the same: measure carefully, then argue for somebody.

The other half of my life is drawing. I've been taking illustration commissions since 2020, designing merchandise alongside it, and posting original character work on Lofter. Blue is my favorite color, and it shows.
